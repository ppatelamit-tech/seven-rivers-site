A different kind of evaluation

Diagnosis is a beginning, not a label

The name Seven Rivers refers to how this practice thinks: several independent streams of understanding, brought together into one river of care.

Time

Your evaluation is two full 60-minute sessions on separate days, and follow-ups are never rushed. Careful psychiatry cannot be done in rushed slots, so this practice does not offer them.

Depth

You are assessed through multiple clinical lenses: biological, psychological, developmental, relational, and lifestyle. Symptoms make sense in context, and the context is where treatment gets traction.

Continuity

You see the same psychiatrist every visit. Your history is not re-explained to a rotating cast. Small panel, direct communication, decisions made with you rather than about you.

A note from Dr. Patel

I built this practice for people who have been assessed but never understood. Whatever brings you here, we will make sense of it together, at a human pace. You do not need to know what is wrong to start. That is what the free consultation is for.

Getting started

From first click to first plan

Free 15-minute consultation

Every new patient starts with a free video consultation. You check the fit; we confirm this is the right level of care. No charge, no obligation.

Complete intake

Before your evaluation, you fill out a structured intake so session time is spent on understanding, not paperwork.

A two-session evaluation

Two full 60-minute sessions on separate days, covering your history, current life, prior treatment, and goals. The time between sessions is part of the method.

A plan in plain language

You leave with a working formulation and a treatment plan you understand, including what we will try, why, and how we will know it is working.

Is this practice right for you?

A good fit matters more than a fast start

Likely a strong fit

Adults in Connecticut seeking evaluation or medication management for anxiety, depression, ADHD, OCD, PTSD, bipolar spectrum conditions, or insomnia. People who want a doctor who explains reasoning, prescribes conservatively, and treats deprescribing as seriously as prescribing.

If you need more than we can offer

This is an outpatient telehealth practice, so it is not the right setting for psychiatric emergencies, active suicidal crisis, conditions needing frequent in-person monitoring, or court-ordered evaluations. If that describes your situation, please still reach out or call 988; we would rather point you toward the right place than leave you searching. The contact page lists resources.

Two currents

Healing and Ascent

Every domain of a life has a down-going and an up-going current. Care here runs in both: healing what hurts, then building what carries you. You choose where to start, the crossing is made together, and the current can change at any visit.

The healing current

Turn toward what hurts

Support and regulation for a nervous system on alert, and the resolution of fear, grief, and shame. Psychiatric treatment, somatic work, and supportive therapy.

The ascent current

Build what carries you

Mental fitness is to mental health what physical fitness is to physical health: not merely the absence of struggle, but trained capacity. Challenge over support, emotional performance built deliberately, and the shedding of what no longer serves you.
