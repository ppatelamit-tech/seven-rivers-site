Telehealth psychiatry · Connecticut

Psychiatry with the depth to understand you

Seven Rivers Psychiatry is the telehealth practice of Amit Patel, MD, a board-certified psychiatrist serving adults across Connecticut. Most psychiatric care starts with a checklist and ends with a prescription in under twenty minutes. This practice starts with your whole story: a free 15-minute consultation, a comprehensive evaluation across two 60-minute sessions, a formulation drawn from seven streams of understanding, and the same psychiatrist at every visit.

Care runs in two currents. Healing is support and regulation for a nervous system on alert, and the resolution of fear, grief, and shame. Ascent is the training of mental fitness: calibrated challenge, trainable emotional performance, and the building of what carries you. You choose where to begin, and the current can change at any visit.

Private pay, with superbills for out-of-network reimbursement and published fees. Read the treatment philosophy, the services and conditions treated, and the fees, or meet Dr. Patel.
